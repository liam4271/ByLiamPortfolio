# The Lovable Lens

Create a high-end, creative portfolio website for my UGC & travel content creator activity.

The website should feel like a professional creative studio / personal brand, not like a generic influencer portfolio. The goal is to make brands, hotels, tourism boards, restaurants and travel-related companies immediately understand my creative universe and feel confident about working with me.

Overall creative direction

Design a website that feels:

Creative but sophisticated

Editorial and visually driven

Modern, minimal and premium

Warm, human and adventurous

Intelligent rather than overly promotional

Inspired by contemporary travel magazines, art direction and independent creative studios

Avoid the typical "influencer portfolio" aesthetic: no excessive gradients, generic glassmorphism, cliché tropical visuals, excessive animations or overly corporate layouts.

Use a strong and distinctive color palette that works beautifully with travel photography and video thumbnails. The colors should create a recognizable visual identity while keeping the content and imagery as the main focus.

Typography should feel editorial and refined, with a strong hierarchy between large headlines, supporting text and body copy.

Design system

Build the website around a consistent, professional design system that is easy to maintain and expand.

Define reusable:

Typography styles

Color tokens

Spacing system

Buttons

Cards

Tags / categories

Navigation

Section headers

Image/video containers

Hover states

Responsive layouts

Keep the components modular so I can easily add new projects, services, images, videos and testimonials later without breaking the design.

The website must be fully responsive, with particular attention to mobile because much of the portfolio content will be visual.

Animations should be subtle and intentional: smooth image reveals, elegant hover interactions, gentle transitions and scrolling effects. Avoid animations that distract from the content.

HERO

Create a visually striking hero section immediately communicating who I am and what I create.

The headline should be short, memorable and intelligent, rather than simply saying "UGC Creator".

Position me as someone who creates authentic visual stories around travel, places and experiences.

Include:

Strong headline

Short supporting statement

Primary CTA: "Work with me"

Secondary CTA: "View selected work"

Strong visual element using photography/video

Minimal navigation

The hero should create an immediate emotional reaction and make visitors want to explore.

ABOUT

Create an editorial-style introduction to me and my creative approach.

The copy should feel personal, confident and authentic without sounding like a CV.

Explain:

Who I am

My relationship with travel and visual storytelling

What makes my approach different

Why brands can benefit from working with me

Use a combination of typography, photography and/or small visual details rather than a simple block of text.

The tone should be intelligent, natural, curious and confident.

SERVICES

Present my services clearly but creatively.

Potential services include:

UGC videos

Travel & destination content

Hotel / hospitality content

Restaurant & food content

Lifestyle content

Short-form videos / Reels / TikTok

Photography

Social media content

Do not make this look like a traditional pricing/services grid.

Create visually engaging service cards with:

Service name

Short description

Type of content delivered

Optional visual example

Subtle interaction

Make it immediately clear what a potential client can hire me for.

SELECTED WORK

This should be the visual centerpiece of the website.

Create a premium editorial portfolio / case-study section showcasing selected projects.

Use large imagery and video thumbnails with an art-directed layout rather than a basic symmetrical grid.

Each project should be presented with:

Project / brand name

Location

Type of project

Short description

Visual content

Optional results / deliverables

Make the portfolio easy to update so I can add new projects without redesigning the page.

Use hover interactions to reveal project information while keeping the visual experience clean. For this section i want something creative and for all the video i want them inside an iphone mokcup like the first image exemple

PROCESS

Explain how I work with brands in a simple and reassuring way.

Create a clear 4–5 step process:

01 — Discovery Understand the brand, audience and objectives.

02 — Creative Direction Develop the concept, visual direction and content ideas.

03 — Production Film and photograph authentic, high-quality content.

04 — Editing Transform the raw material into polished, platform-ready content.

05 — Delivery Provide the final assets ready to be used across social media and digital channels.

The section should communicate professionalism, reliability and creativity without becoming overly corporate.

CONTACT

Finish with a strong, memorable CTA.

Instead of simply saying "Contact me", create a statement that encourages brands to start a conversation.

For example, use a tone similar to:

"Have a place worth telling a story about?"

or

"Let's turn your next destination into a story."

Include:

Short CTA statement

Email

Social links

Contact form

Clear "Work with me" button

The final section should feel like the natural conclusion of the visual story.

Content & copywriting

Write all initial website copy for me.

The writing should be:

Catchy but not cheesy

Intelligent and concise

Personal but professional

Editorial rather than corporate

Confident without exaggerating

Focused on storytelling, travel, places and experiences

Avoid generic phrases such as: "Passionate content creator" "I bring your vision to life" "Taking your brand to the next level" "Creating engaging content that converts"

The copy should feel distinctive and human, as if it belongs to a real creative person with a strong point of view.

UX & technical requirements

Build the website with:

Excellent desktop and mobile responsive behavior

Fast loading

Accessible typography and contrast

Clean navigation

Sticky navigation that changes subtly on scroll

Smooth but restrained transitions

Reusable components

Easy-to-edit content

Clear separation between content and layout

A scalable design system

Structure the code so that I can easily maintain the portfolio and add new projects later.

The final result should feel closer to a high-end creative portfolio / travel editorial website than a conventional UGC creator website.
